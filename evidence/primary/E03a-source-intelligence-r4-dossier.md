## Implication for the premise — our assessment

The finding makes constructive, time-dependent visual processing scientifically plausible and supplies a concrete intervention/readout family. It does not show that the Dynamic Door description is necessary. Conventional recurrent visual inference is itself a strong surviving explanation. A fair competitor must therefore include recurrent computation, rather than treating a purely feedforward model as the only alternative.

The transfer to Paper II also has a precise boundary: within-trial visual completion is not automatically across-trial history dependence, experiential identity or H/A selectivity. A masking intervention changes incoming stimulation. Its timing effects cannot simply be renamed a selective intervention on the proposed accessibility mechanism.
