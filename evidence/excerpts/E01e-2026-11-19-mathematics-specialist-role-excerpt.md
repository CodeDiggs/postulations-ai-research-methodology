## 4. Two valid forms of progress

**A. Foundational mathematical progress.** A genuinely useful conditional theorem, well-defined new operator, sharp counterexample, prior-art equivalence, rigorous impossibility proof, simplification or quantified model limitation is worthwhile even if no immediate experiment follows. Label it **mathematical**, not empirical.

**B. Scientific-decision progress.** Prefer prospective quantitative differences between a restricted proposed mechanism and a strong ordinary model, with a measurable endpoint, calibrated nuisance assumptions, source chronology, falsification criterion, identifiability and feasible data/apparatus. Do not fabricate a unique prediction where models are observationally equivalent.

Respect the project's existing **two-consecutive-math-only-iterations** pivot rule: without new decision-relevant consequences or an independently valuable mathematical closure, move to a concrete test/source requirement, adversarial review, or honest `NON-IDENTIFIABLE/BLOCKED` stop. Avoid both the **equation treadmill** and the **source-audit treadmill**.
