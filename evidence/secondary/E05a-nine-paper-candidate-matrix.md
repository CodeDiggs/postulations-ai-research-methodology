# Nine-paper evidence matrix — bounded candidate sample

**Matrix ID:** AI-MATRIX-20261118-v1. Prepared 2026-11-18 by the methodology coordinator.

**Sampling frame:** Exactly one candidate per Paper I–IX from the [November 15 06:12 EST review-only checkpoint](https://github.com/CodeDiggs/postulations-dynamic-door/blob/main/coordination/checkpoints/2026-11-15-0612-review-only.md). All nine candidate files were **directly fetched at the exact source PR heads** during this matrix audit; PR metadata was inspected for the initially uninspected five plus the relevant remaining proposals. The underlying code and raw data were **not independently executed** for this matrix. All nine PRs were open, draft, and unmerged when checked. This is a purposive cross-sectional document sample, not a random sample, nine independent studies, or evidence of project-wide effectiveness.

## Coding rules

- **Math/synthetic** means conditional mathematical analysis, exact enumeration or reported self-run tests, not empirical discovery.
- **Primary inspection** means the candidate's original file was fetched at a fixed Git commit; it does not mean proof verification or original code/data replay.
- **Rival** is the strongest alternative expressly preserved in the examined candidate/checkpoint, not necessarily every viable scientific explanation.
- **Adoption** means accepted authoritative scientific paper/interface/main/owner decision; an open draft PR or coordinator annotation does not qualify.
- **Human/physical DD evidence** requires a direct empirical observation that discriminates a specified DD claim from suitable conventional alternatives, with independently verified measurement/controls. Published ordinary human data about perception or culture is not automatically such evidence.

## What the matrix establishes and does not establish

This bounded set exposes **nine documented candidate reports**, multiple explicit rival models and retained failures, and **no claimed distinctive physical/human DD validation or adoption in the examined proposals**. Those are properties of the selected records as inspected, not population frequencies, not definitive truth assessments, and not evidence that every paper has equal research maturity. The review-only checkpoint and candidate files may share authorship and assumptions; the nine rows cannot be treated as independent trials.

**Not measured:** author effort, token/computation cost, all experiment denominators, complete failures and abandoned investigations, external peer-review accuracy, prospective efficacy, exact downstream adoption, or scientific novelty. The matrix is a methodological *coding instrument* and a baseline inventory, not a causal comparison of AI research methods.
